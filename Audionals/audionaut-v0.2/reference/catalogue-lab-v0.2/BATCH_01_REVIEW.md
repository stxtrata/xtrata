## Listening pass

Load a source, adopt or change the note-based classification, and listen once in full. Identify a usable region. For a loop, repeat the region and compare its duration with a manually counted beat count. Verify the seam, tempo and any pitch label separately. Save multiple clips when one inscription contains different usable sounds.
