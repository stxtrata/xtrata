Xtrata Chess · Launch Canary

From nothing to a played first move, every transaction signed by your wallet.

No seed phrase is ever asked for, entered, or stored. This page builds each request, shows it to you in full, and hands it to your wallet to sign. It holds no keys and can sign nothing by itself. Nothing is sent without a click, and the deploy step asks you to type the contract name first.

On mainnet this spends real STX and cannot be undone. A contract name, once used, is gone forever.
This page was opened from a file, not a server. Wallet extensions do not inject into file:// pages, so Connect will find nothing. Serve it over localhost instead:

cd xtrata-chess && npm run dev
then open http://localhost:4321/dist/xtrata-chess-launch-canary.html
