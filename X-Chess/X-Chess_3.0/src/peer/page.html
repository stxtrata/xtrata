Game rules and connection limits

This mode has separate, unranked history. Checkmate, the standard automatic chess draws, a signed resignation, or an accepted draw offer can finish a record. Resign and offer a draw on your turn; a draw offer travels with your next move. Local clocks are advisory: expiry and disconnection never prove a loss. Conflicting signed branches require review.

Pairing uses a manual invitation and response. Direct connectivity depends on your networks. You may configure STUN or TURN, or exchange saved game records manually. Keep this browser’s storage and stay on the same origin: it holds your non-exportable game key. Your game key cannot spend wallet funds. Share connection details privately; they may contain network addresses.
