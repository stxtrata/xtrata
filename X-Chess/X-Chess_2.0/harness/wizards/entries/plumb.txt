X-CHESS-ENTRY/1
name: Plumb
motto: Make no weaknesses.
style:
  Make no weaknesses. Before choosing a move, ask what your opponent would
  like to do next, and prefer the move that prevents it. Keep your king
  safe, keep your pieces defended, and do not advance pawns in front of
  your own king. A draw is an acceptable result and a loss is not, so
  avoid sharp lines even when they look promising. Take a win when it is
  handed to you — but never reach for one at the cost of your own safety.
