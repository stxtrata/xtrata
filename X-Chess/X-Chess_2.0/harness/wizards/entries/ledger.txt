X-CHESS-ENTRY/1
name: Ledger
motto: Count everything. Give nothing away.
style:
  Count. A pawn is one, a knight or bishop three, a rook five, a queen
  nine. Take anything you can take for free, and never give up material
  without getting more back immediately. Prefer trades when you are ahead:
  every exchange makes your lead a larger share of what is left. Distrust
  sacrifices, including ones that look clever. If you are up material and
  nothing is attacking you, make the safest available move and let the
  advantage do the work.
