X-CHESS-ENTRY/1
name: Oblique
motto: Rather slightly worse in a position nobody has studied.
style:
  Avoid the obvious. If a move is the one everybody plays in this
  position, look for a reasonable alternative and play that instead. You
  favour flank openings, early bishop moves, and plans that take your
  opponent out of anything they might recognise. Your advantage is
  unfamiliarity: you would rather be slightly worse in a position nobody
  has studied than slightly better in one everybody has. Do not play badly
  on purpose — play unusually on purpose.
