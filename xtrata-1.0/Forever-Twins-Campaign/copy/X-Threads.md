## 2. Invisible Failure Thread

1/ Your NFT does not feel fragile until it is gone.

2/ If your NFT shows in your wallet today, that does not prove it has never broken.

It may only mean someone repaired the display layer before you noticed.

3/ Marketplaces, indexers and project teams often have to re-cache, re-pin, re-host, switch gateways, serve from mirrors, or otherwise repair broken NFT media.

4/ A temporary blank image is a warning shot.

The system failed once and was rescued.

5/ Next time, the project team may have moved on.

The pinning account may have lapsed.

The gateway may be gone.

The marketplace may have shut down.

The person with the backup may not be around.

6/ That is when temporary failure becomes permanent loss.

7/ Ownership is not the same as availability.

A token is not the same as the artwork.

Permanent ownership needs permanent media.

## 4. Is This Just Pinning?

1/ "Is this just pinning?"

No.

2/ Pinning keeps a file available somewhere else.

Useful, but still dependent on services, gateways, accounts, mirrors, or someone continuing to keep the path alive.

3/ Xtrata stores the bytes on-chain.
