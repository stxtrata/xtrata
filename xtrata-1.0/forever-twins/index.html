The model

One permanent twin, bound to your original

Most NFTs don't store their art — they point to a file on a server or gateway that can disappear. Forever Twins fix that for collections that already exist, without asking holders to migrate anything.

1

Inscribe

The original token's artwork is written permanently and fully on-chain via Xtrata, and a forever twin is bound to it 1:1.

2

Bound & inseparable

One of the pair is always escrowed in the contract; the holder always keeps the other. They can be swapped, but never split apart.

3

Finalized forever

Each twin carries a canonical content hash. Once finalized, that record is immutable — independent of any hosting bill, gateway, or company.
