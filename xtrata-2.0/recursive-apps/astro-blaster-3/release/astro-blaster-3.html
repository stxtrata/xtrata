    How to play

    Controls

    Arrows/WASD move · fire is automatic

    X/Shift/Space Nova bomb · P pause

    Touch: drag anywhere to steer; the ship follows your finger with an offset. Tap NOVA for a bomb.

    Gamepad: stick or d-pad to move, A/B bomb, Start pause.

    Scoring

    Chain: keep killing within 1.5 s to raise your multiplier up to x8. A hit resets it.

    Graze: let bullets pass close to your ship for points and Nova charge. Only the white dot is your hitbox.

    Perfect wave and fast boss kills pay bonuses. Each Deep Loop multiplies everything by 1.5.

    The run

    6 sectors of 4 waves plus a boss. Pick 1 of 3 upgrades after each wave, and spend scrap at the Dock after each boss.

    Beat all six sectors and the Deep Loop begins: harder, faster, richer.

    Daily run: same seed for everyone for one Bitcoin day (144 blocks), with its own board.
