# Xtrata x Zero Authority DAO - Sponsored Bounty Proposals

Prepared for Emmanuel (Grimaldo) to forward to Zero Authority DAO.

## How this fits Zero Authority

Zero Authority bounties work like this: a steward posts a problem with a title, description, dates, and a reward. The reward is locked in a Clarity smart contract, contributors submit work, and the winner(s) are paid out onchain. Recent bounties have ranged from roughly 100 to 250 STX, sometimes split across multiple winners, and creative bounties (video, art, content) get the most submissions.

That shape suits Xtrata well: every submission is an inscription, so the proof of work IS the deliverable, visible onchain. Below are three bounty options. Each can run standalone, or Zero Authority can pick their favorite. Suggested rewards assume roughly 150 to 300 STX per bounty, split across winners to maximize participation.

---

## Option 1: "Inscribe Your First Masterpiece" (creative, broadest reach)

**Goal:** Get as many people as possible through the Xtrata Inscription Wizard for the first time.

**The task:** Inscribe an original piece of media on Stacks using Xtrata: a song, artwork, photo, poem, or short video. Share the inscription link and a screenshot or short post about the experience on X or in the Stacks community.

**Judging:** Creativity and quality of the inscribed piece, plus quality of the public share.

**Suggested reward:** 200 STX split as 100 / 50 / 25 / 25 (top piece, runner up, two community picks). Optionally add small participation prizes for every valid inscription to drive volume.

**Why this one:** Lowest barrier, most submissions, produces real onchain content and social proof simultaneously. Creative bounties historically get the highest engagement on Zero Authority.

---

## Option 2: "The Collection Challenge" (power users, showcases the wizard)

**Goal:** Showcase the Inscription Wizard's collection feature, Xtrata's key differentiator.

**The task:** Use the Inscription Wizard to inscribe a themed collection of 5 to 10 pieces (music EP, art series, photo essay, generative set). Submit the collection link plus a short writeup or thread explaining the theme and the process. Activate the receipt mechanism to generate a receipt that shows the batch inscription details.

**Judging:** Cohesion of the collection, quality, and how well the writeup demonstrates the wizard to others. Inscription of batch receipt.

**Suggested reward:** 250 STX split 150 / 100 for the top two collections.

**Why this one:** Fewer but deeper submissions. Each winning entry doubles as a tutorial and marketing asset for the collection flow.

---

## Option 3: "Build on Xtrata" (developers, recursive inscriptions)

**Goal:** Attract builders and prove Xtrata as a platform, not just a tool.

**The task:** Inscribe a recursive application or interactive piece using Xtrata: an app, game, generative artwork, or tool that references other inscriptions. Open source encouraged. Submit the inscription link plus a short demo video or writeup.

**Judging:** Technical creativity, use of recursion, and usability.

**Suggested reward:** 300 STX, single winner or 200 / 100 split, reflecting the higher effort.

**Why this one:** Highest signal per submission. Recursive apps are the strongest demonstration of what inscriptions on Stacks can do, and winners often become long term ecosystem builders.

---

## Recommended approach

If Zero Authority will run only one, start with Option 1 for reach, then follow with Option 3 once there is an active audience. If budget allows, run 1 and 3 together as a "create and build" pair covering both creators and developers.

Simple engagement tasks (join the Telegram, follow the account) are better handled as quests or as a small bonus criterion inside a bounty, not as bounties themselves. Onchain bounty submissions should produce onchain work.

**Logistics Xtrata will provide:** a short "first inscription" walkthrough link, judging support, and amplification of winning entries. Timeline suggestion: 2 to 3 weeks per bounty.
