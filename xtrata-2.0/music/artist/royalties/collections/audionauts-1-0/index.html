A receiving wallet is the current holder of the artist’s earliest song inscription in this collection’s song list. Each minted edition earns one 5 STX royalty for its primary secret-song artist. Bonus songs and playlists do not add another royalty.

“Paid · retained by Xtrata” marks royalties kept by Xtrata when it holds the artist’s receiving song. Where verified, the Audionaut sale links to incoming STX as supporting evidence. “No receipt” means a payment has not been recorded here. Confirmed receipts include a transaction link and the address that held the artist’s receiving song when paid. Mint and ownership checks use a confirmed block, so recent activity can take a little time to appear.

Automatic payment tracking

New receipts with an edition memo, such as audionaut:84, are verified every five minutes. For several editions, use audionaut:2,5 and transfer 5 STX per edition to their shared receiving wallet.

Add the public paying wallet addresses to discover older transfers, including payments to former holders. Transfers without an edition memo need their covered editions assigned once.
