# Xtrata Funding Radar

Grants, hackathons, bounties and funding programmes Xtrata could apply for.

Maintained automatically by the **Xtrata funding radar** routine, which runs every
Monday at 09:05 and commits to `main-staging`. Newest week first. Entries are only
added when a programme is a genuine fit and its link and deadline have been
verified. Anything unconfirmed is listed separately rather than presented as fact.

Manage the routine at <https://claude.ai/code/routines/trig_01Yc3arqEa7AUkjEGsUEXs82>.

Scope: Stacks Endowment funds, Stacks Foundation grants and Ascent, future DeGrants
cohorts and Zero Authority DAO, Bitcoin and Ordinals ecosystem grants, digital
preservation and cultural heritage funding, generative art and on-chain art grants
and residencies, and relevant hackathons and bounties.
