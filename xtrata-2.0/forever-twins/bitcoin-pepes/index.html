How It Works

Three steps. We do the first two for you.

01

Pick a Pepe

Type any Bitcoin Pepe number. We load it straight from the chain and show you the artwork — browse as many as you like.

02

We verify it

Behind the scenes we fetch the Pepe’s real image and confirm it matches the canonical hash sealed on-chain. No files to upload.

03

Inscribe its twin

One click opens your wallet to create the permanent Xtrata twin. Later, the Pepe’s owner can swap which side stays liquid.
