Rules and answers

Everything you need to know.

Who can enter?
Anyone able to use a compatible Stacks wallet and meet the requirements shown on the official Zero Authority bounty page.
What makes an entry valid?
  • The work must be original and you must control the necessary rights.
  • It must be successfully inscribed through Xtrata during the bounty period.
  • You must provide a publicly accessible inscription link and public post.
  • The complete entry must be submitted through Zero Authority before the deadline.
Can I enter something I made previously?
Yes, provided it is your original work and the Xtrata inscription itself is created during the campaign period.
Can I submit more than one piece?
You may create and inscribe more than one work, but only one official competition entry per wallet is recommended unless the official bounty rules state otherwise.
Does social engagement determine the winner?
No. Likes, followers, and repost counts do not determine the result. The public share is judged for clarity and storytelling, not popularity.
Can collaborative work be entered?
Yes. One wallet must be nominated to receive any reward, and collaborators are responsible for agreeing how a prize would be divided.
How are rewards paid?
Rewards are distributed through the official Zero Authority bounty process to the nominated Stacks wallet.
Is there an entry fee?
There is no separate competition entry fee. Any inscription cost or sponsored inscription arrangement should be confirmed in the official bounty details before launch.
