1. Create and protect your wallet

Create one disposable wallet for this browser. Your password encrypts it locally. Keep the backup and password safe: clearing browser data without a backup can lose your funds. An unlocked browser wallet is only suitable for a small test balance.

Before funding, download your backup and select that file here to verify it using your password. No seed is printed or sent to a server.

2. Add your test balance

After verifying your backup, send 1 STX on Stacks mainnet to this address. Your sending wallet charges its own transfer fee. Funding does not authorise spending.

3. Choose your song and fee

Test a direct master inscription. The master holder at execution receives 0.00005 STX. Albums and inferred master pointers are not supported in this canary. Payments and listening-wallet activity are public.

Fees never increase automatically. 0.0002 STX is below the 257-byte transaction’s 1 microSTX/byte baseline and may be rejected. A low fee can remain pending. This is a measured baseline, not a guarantee of confirmation.

No song loaded

Paid starts require audio to start unmuted. Pausing, buffering and seeking do not charge again. Skipping after it starts keeps that charge. A new song or natural loop is another paid start. No proof of listening duration is claimed.

4. Try a small automatic session

Preview and approve a limit once, then let the playlist play. If a payment is unresolved when the next song starts, paid recording stops and music continues free. No backlog of catch-up charges.

No spending authorised. Already-broadcast transactions can still confirm after Stop or Lock.

5. Payment history and recovery

Track requested and actual fees, confirmation times and recipients. Reconcile an uncertain payment before starting another. Duplicate submission uses identical signed bytes, never a fresh payment.

Stop testing and resolve pending transactions first. The full amount and fee are reviewed before signing. Very small balances may not cover a withdrawal fee.

Advanced: offline safety checks

These checks make no blockchain calls, create no wallet and spend nothing. They check exact currency conversion, session limits and that reports exclude signed transaction bytes.
