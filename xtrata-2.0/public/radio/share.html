How it works

Paste the code into your site's custom HTML or embed block. Keep the player at least 240 pixels wide. Listeners load the player, then press play. Each listener gets their own rotation of music.

This is a browser music player, not a continuous MP3 or HLS broadcast URL. A plain <audio src="…"> cannot play the station. Use the minimal iframe when you only need audio controls.

Audio is retrieved through Xtrata's hosted inscription services. Browser privacy settings may limit saved likes and playback history. Your site's Content Security Policy must permit this site's origin in frame-src.
