    HOW TO MOVE

    • WASD or arrow keys walk, Shift runs. Click the room once and the mouse looks around by itself (the pointer disappears). Esc or Q gives the pointer back for the buttons; click the room to look again.
    • Same as the casino: E, Enter or a click on a cabinet connects you to it where you stand. You can keep walking and looking, and you disconnect when you walk away (E or WALK AWAY too).
    • G opens the list of machines and walks you to any of them.

    AT A CABINET

    • Space spins, [ ] change the bet, T turbo, M sound, 1–9 picks in a bonus. Q or MORE shows every other control (auto, guide, Bonus Lab, full screen) and frees the pointer.
    • Put the crosshair on the cabinet's own buttons, lever or screen and click. Turbo, gamble and bonus buttons are also drawn on the screen itself.
    • V or CABINET VIEW looks at the whole cabinet, then a close-up of the screen, with the pointer free; PLAY HERE takes you back to where you stood.
    • F or FULL SCREEN fills the window with the game's own layout, with every menu, panel and setting on its bottom bar.
    • L or BONUS LAB opens the machine's Bonus Lab (or its test panel) so you can pick any bonus round and play it now.
