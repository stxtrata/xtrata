Questions

Why does my wallet show…

I inscribed but I have no tickets

Tickets only count for the wallet that made the inscription. Check that you inscribed through Xtrata on or after 1 October with the same wallet you are searching, then press Refresh. New actions appear a few minutes after they confirm on chain.

My wallet is not listed

The board lists every wallet that has made an inscription, collected an Audionaut or used a ticket action since 1 October. If you have done none of these with that wallet yet, it will not appear. A wallet that has not inscribed has no tickets in the draw.

Why are my tickets "held"?

An inscription (1 to 5 tickets by size) or an Audionaut (5 tickets) puts you in the draw and unlocks the rest. Until your wallet has one, other actions are counted but not entered. They switch on the moment you inscribe or collect.

How many tickets is an inscription worth?

It depends on the size of the file. Up to 1 MB (1,048,576 bytes) is 1 ticket, over 1 MB is 2, over 2 MB is 3, over 3 MB is 4, and over 4 MB is 5. Five is the most one inscription can earn, however large the file. Every inscription you make from 1 October is scored on its own and added up. If the page cannot see an inscription's size yet it counts 1 ticket until the next full read of the chain.

Do repeat actions add more tickets?

Each different action adds one ticket, so playing ten songs is one ticket. Inscriptions are the exception: every inscription earns its own tickets by size, so ten inscribed songs earn at least ten. Collecting Audionauts is worth 5 tickets per wallet, however many you collect: one Audionaut or ten, it is 5. Every high score you commit on-chain is its own ticket.

I use more than one wallet

Only one wallet per X account counts, and it must be the one you announced. Do everything with that wallet.

Where do winners get announced?

Weekly raffle winners are announced in the Xtrata Telegram. Main prizes and special awards are announced on Wednesday 28 October.
