trataPreservation checker

Where does your NFT's art actually live?

Paste a link, a contract address or a wallet address. We'll tell you what we know about where the art is stored, what looks good and what worries us.

A wallet address is public, so there is nothing to connect or sign. We don't keep your address or what you paste; we only keep anonymous daily counts of which collections get looked up. Names ending in .btc aren't supported yet; use the address that starts with SP.

What this does and doesn't tell you
  • This is about whether the art and its description can still be found. It is not about the safety, price or custody of the token itself.
  • Our checks are samples, run on the dates shown on each card. A collection can change after that date.
  • Collections we haven't tested aren't "fine". They're just not covered yet.
