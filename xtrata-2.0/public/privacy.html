Xtrata

Privacy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 20 August 2026

Xtrata is a data layer for Bitcoin Layer 2 (Stacks) and the website at xtrata.xyz that reads and writes it. This page says what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and — importantly — what can and cannot be deleted.

You do not need an account to use Xtrata. Most of the site works with no identifier at all: no sign-up, no email, no profile.

The part to read first: permanence

Anything inscribed on-chain is permanent, public, and cannot be deleted by us or by anyone else. That is the point of the technology, and it is not reversible.

Before you inscribe, assume the content and the address that paid for it are readable by anyone, forever. No deletion request can undo an inscription. If you would not want something public in ten years, do not inscribe it.

Nothing described in the rest of this page is inscribed on-chain. Diagnostics, sessions and anything read from a connected account stay in our ordinary databases, are deletable, and are deleted on the schedules below.

What the website collects

Your wallet

When you connect a Stacks wallet, your public address is used in your browser to show your holdings and to build transactions you approve yourself. Private keys and seed phrases never reach our servers. We cannot see them, and we cannot move your assets.

Diagnostics

We record what goes wrong on the site so it can be fixed. Each event holds a random session id, the page path, which step failed, an error code and scrubbed message, a coarse browser / operating system / device type, and the country your request came from.

If a wallet is connected we store a salted hash of the address — sha256(secret salt + address) — so failures can be followed across a single journey. The salt is a server secret, which means the stored value cannot be reversed back to your address or brute-forced. No raw or partial address is kept.

Retention: routine events are deleted after 90 days, error records after 180 days. This happens automatically.

Xtrata Radio

When a track will not play, the player reports the contract and token id and the reason. That record contains no identifier of any kind — it describes the track, not the listener — and exists so nobody else has to hit the same broken track.

Likes are stored in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Creator tools

If you publish a collection, we store the Stacks address that created it, the collection metadata you enter, and any preview assets you upload. These are kept while the collection exists and are removed when you delete it.

Signing in with Dataing

This applies only if you choose to connect a Dataing account. It is entirely optional, and every other part of Xtrata works without it.

What we ask for

  • Sign-in and basic profile — to know a session belongs to you.
  • Wallet status — whether you already have an active Dataing wallet, so we know whether to offer you anything that needs one.
  • Monetization preference — your own choice about how apps may monetise for you, so we can respect it.

We use these to build you a radio station from music already inscribed on-chain. That is the whole purpose.

Where the access token lives

A Dataing token is a credential to somebody's dating profile, so it is treated as one. The token is never given to your browser. The exchange happens on our server, the token is stored server-side, and your browser receives only a random session id in a cookie that JavaScript cannot read. The token is never written into a page, a URL, an analytics event, or a log line.

Retention: the session expires when the token does — typically within an hour — and the stored row is deleted.

What we never do

  • We never inscribe anything from Dataing on-chain — not your profile, not your preferences, and never anything about who you matched or spoke with.
  • We never store your matches, messages, or photos.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for advertising.
  • We cannot write to your Dataing profile. The connection is read-only.

Disconnecting

Signing out of Xtrata ends the session and deletes the stored token. You can also revoke Xtrata's access from inside Dataing at any time, which stops it regardless of anything we do.

Who else is involved

  • Hiro — reading data off-chain-to-browser means your browser talks to a public Stacks node (api.mainnet.hiro.so) directly. Your IP address is visible to them, as it would be to any website you visit. Nothing about you is added to those requests.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and its databases, and keeps standard, short-lived request logs.

There are no advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party scripts collecting behaviour on this site.

Cookies

Only functional ones. Nothing for advertising or cross-site tracking:

  • xt_dataing_state and xt_dataing_verifier — protect the sign-in handshake from tampering. Deleted the moment sign-in finishes; expire in 10 minutes regardless.
  • xt_dataing_session — your opaque session id, if you connected Dataing.

Your rights, and their honest limits

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to how it is used. Write to privacy@xtrata.xyz and we will answer within 30 days.

Two limits we would rather state plainly than have you discover:

  • On-chain data cannot be deleted. Not by request, not by court order, not by us. See the top of this page.
  • Salted diagnostic hashes cannot be searched by address, because they are not reversible. That is a deliberate privacy property, but it means we usually cannot single out "your" diagnostic rows. They delete themselves on the schedule above.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our answer, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Xtrata is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the version and date above change and the new version is published here before it takes effect.
