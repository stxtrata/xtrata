Make your first beat

  1. On a first phone visit, Mobile opens with a starter session. Tap Play to hear it. A returning session opens your saved song and workspace.
  2. Choose a sample under Track. Use Solo to hear it during playback or Preview for a single hit. Mute, Level, Inserts and Effects belong to that track. Choosing an instrument reveals its synth picker, synth panel and piano roll. Each row of four pads on a phone represents one beat. A lit pad triggers that sound. Tap an empty pad to add a hit or tap a lit pad to remove it.
  3. The pads follow playback from bar to bar and sequence to sequence. Turn Follow playback off, or choose a bar or sequence while playing, to keep editing there. Turn it back on to catch up. Loop bar repeats the playing bar at its end. Tap a different bar to play it on the next pass. Loop sequence works the same way for sequences. Play song carries on from the current beat. While stopped, these buttons start playback from your selection. In an arranged Timeline the pads follow the selected channel’s pattern clip; a position without a single pattern shows the Timeline bar.
  4. Choose Add beat to browse. Tap Preview on a row to hear that beat alone at your song tempo. A playing song continues silently while you listen. Stop the preview or close Beats to return to the mix. Apply beat layers it into the named sequence at your song tempo. Applying another beat replaces untouched parts of that layer. Edited beat tracks and your other recordings and synths stay in the song. Use Undo to reverse an application. For a complete song template, open Project and choose a starter song. Use New for a blank project.

To build a loop by ear, tap Loop bar and tap a coloured Track pad to switch sounds while it plays. A white outline marks your selected track. Tap a dim pad marked M to select and unmute only that track, or choose it in the Track list to edit it silently. A hollow light shows a track outside the solo mix. Use the bank arrows for more than 16 tracks. Tap Track pads to fold the bank. Tap Groups, name a group such as Drums, tick the tracks you want and tap Save group. Tap its name to cycle through just those tracks, or All tracks to browse the whole song. The Mute beside each name lets you drop that group out and bring it back while editing any track. An overlapping group keeps its members muted when another group is released. An individual track pad can override its own mute. Individual mute choices are preserved. Groups are saved with the song.

Help → Guided lessons highlights the real controls as you work. End a lesson at any point. Its edits are part of your song and can be undone.

Load, trim and layer samples

  1. Choose the destination under Track, then tap Load sound. In Library, choose a category and sound. Audition it, then tap Load. The other tabs accept a Bitcoin inscription, Xtrata ID, URL or local file.
  2. Tap Trim to set this channel’s start and end. Preview the result and choose Done. To change just one hit, turn on Edit step, then tap that pad.
  3. Choose a second channel and add hits at the same positions to layer the sounds. Turn on Accent to enter accented hits. Turn it off to resume ordinary step editing.
  4. Open Effects, select a processor and add it. Adjust its settings, then choose Done. Open Mixer to balance the layers.

Build a longer arrangement

Sequences edits one four-bar phrase. Switch between the step grid and waveforms with the view button. Choose another sequence or add one with the sequence selector. Copy and paste a phrase from Edit to create a variation.

On a touch device, Timeline opens in Scroll. Swipe across waveforms to move through time or up and down to reach other tracks. Tap Edit to move and trim regions or edit visible automation. Track controls expands the track names into channel strips. Tap it again to give the waveforms more room.

Timeline shows the song across its sequences. Scroll along the ruler to find a section, drag a region to move it, or drag either edge to trim it. Set snap and zoom for finer edits. An existing custom arrangement retains its placements when you switch views.

Balance and automate a mix

Open Mixer and play the song. Use a channel fader for level and pan for placement. Double-click a fader for unity gain or pan to centre it. Scroll the channel bank sideways to reach the other tracks. Watch Master for peaks as you add layers.

In Timeline, reveal Automation and open a track’s parameter menu. Each insert and effect has its own numbered group. Choose a control such as filter cutoff or delay mix, then tap the line to add points. Mode and switch controls change at their points. A send amount affects its own track. Controls under Shared delay return or Shared reverb return affect every track feeding that return. Edit its points or open More views → Automation for detailed control. Choose the track, choose a parameter under New lane, then tap Add lane. Hide the overlay to edit regions again. Use the lane’s read or bypass control to change whether its automation plays.

Play an instrument or record a take

Select an instrument and tap Play live in Mobile or the synth panel. When Ready appears, play the keys or labelled Drum Rack pads. Tap a coloured instrument button to switch sounds while the song plays. Tap the star to bring a favourite to the front of the strip. Sustain holds notes until you tap it again. If audio is interrupted, tap Tap to resume.

Show sets how many octaves you see. Auto fits one or two, and a fixed span stays the same when you rotate. Use − Oct and + Oct to move through the notes. Edit synth opens the instrument controls. Exit full screen docks the keys below the editor and Done closes the keyboard. You can also open the docked keys directly with Expand keyboard.

Open Play setup to select the live instrument and configure MIDI, swing or the arpeggiator. Panic releases sounding notes. In Sequences, open an instrument’s panel to play its keyboard or edit the sound.

Tap ● REC on the synth track, selected synth panel or full-screen keyboard to arm MIDI recording. When stopped, the first played note starts and records the take. When playing, notes are recorded in time; tap REC again to disarm without stopping the song. Choose the Quantize grid and strength, then Apply to take to change an existing MIDI performance. Open its piano roll and use Apply to notes for selected or all notes. Off restores raw timing. Press Play for the configured count-in.

Open More views → Record & takes for audio input, monitoring and takes. Choose the destination and input before recording. Use headphones if you monitor a microphone. The Sampler workspace provides slicing and processing for recorded or loaded audio.

Save, restore or open an older song

Project → Save downloads an editable file. Projects shows recovery versions saved in this browser for this edition. To move a song between devices or editions, download a portable project that includes its local audio.

Project → Load accepts AudioNaut projects and supported older Audional JSON, gzip and AUDX files. The importer chooses the historical playback rules automatically. If a sample cannot be retrieved, use the import report to identify the source and retry it.

Export offers a stereo mix and stems. Choose the editable project format if the recipient needs to continue producing the song.

Prepare on-chain song instructions

Open More views → On-chain publishing. Review the dependencies and their references. For an instruction song, each required sound and the playback runtime must be available from its verified chain reference. Resolve missing dependencies, choose Verify and prepare song, and download the instructions to check them before reviewing an inscription.

For detailed formats and constraints, see the chain device reference, historical import reference and instrument reference.
