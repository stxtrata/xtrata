Make your first beat

  1. On a first phone visit, Mobile opens with a starter session. Tap Play to hear it. A returning session opens your saved song and workspace.
  2. Choose a sample channel. Each row of four pads on a phone represents one beat. A lit pad triggers that sound. Tap an empty pad to add a hit or tap a lit pad to remove it.
  3. The pads follow playback from bar to bar and sequence to sequence. Turn Follow playback off, or choose a bar or sequence while playing, to keep editing there. Turn it back on to catch up. Loop bar and Loop sequence jump playback to your edit. Play song continues through the song from that bar. In an arranged Timeline the pads follow the selected channel’s pattern clip; a position without a single pattern shows the Timeline bar.
  4. Choose Add beat to browse. Apply beat layers it into the named sequence at your song tempo. Applying another beat replaces untouched parts of that layer. Edited beat tracks and your other recordings and synths stay in the song. Use Undo to reverse an application. For a complete song template, open Project and choose a starter song. Use New for a blank project.

Help → Guided lessons highlights the real controls as you work. End a lesson at any point. Its edits are part of your song and can be undone.

Load, trim and layer samples

  1. Choose the destination under Sample channel, then tap Load sound. In Library, choose a category and sound. Audition it, then tap Load. The other tabs accept a Bitcoin inscription, Xtrata ID, URL or local file.
  2. Tap Trim sound to set this channel’s start and end. Preview the result and choose Done. To change just one hit, turn on Edit step, then tap that pad.
  3. Choose a second channel and add hits at the same positions to layer the sounds. Turn on Accent to enter accented hits. Turn it off to resume ordinary step editing.
  4. Open Effects, select a processor and add it. Adjust its settings, then choose Done. Open Mixer to balance the layers.

Build a longer arrangement

Sequences edits one four-bar phrase. Switch between the step grid and waveforms with the view button. Choose another sequence or add one with the sequence selector. Copy and paste a phrase from Edit to create a variation.

Timeline shows the song across its sequences. Scroll along the ruler to find a section, drag a region to move it, or drag either edge to trim it. Set snap and zoom for finer edits. An existing custom arrangement retains its placements when you switch views.

Balance and automate a mix

Open Mixer and play the song. Use a channel fader for level and pan for placement. Double-click a fader for unity gain or pan to centre it. Scroll the channel bank sideways to reach the other tracks. Watch Master for peaks as you add layers.

In Timeline, reveal Automation and choose a track parameter. Edit its points or open More views → Automation for detailed control. Hide the overlay to edit regions again. Use the lane’s read or bypass control to change whether its automation plays.

Play an instrument or record a take

Open Play setup to select the live instrument and configure MIDI, swing or the arpeggiator. Panic releases sounding notes. In Sequences, open an instrument’s panel to play its keyboard or edit the sound.

Open More views → Record & takes for audio input, monitoring and takes. Choose the destination and input before recording. Use headphones if you monitor a microphone. The Sampler workspace provides slicing and processing for recorded or loaded audio.

Save, restore or open an older song

Project → Save downloads an editable file. Projects shows recovery versions saved in this browser for this edition. To move a song between devices or editions, download a portable project that includes its local audio.

Project → Load accepts AudioNaut projects and supported older Audional JSON, gzip and AUDX files. The importer chooses the historical playback rules automatically. If a sample cannot be retrieved, use the import report to identify the source and retry it.

Export offers a stereo mix and stems. Choose the editable project format if the recipient needs to continue producing the song.

Prepare on-chain song instructions

Open More views → On-chain publishing. Review the dependencies and their references. For an instruction song, each required sound and the playback runtime must be available from its verified chain reference. Resolve missing dependencies, choose Verify and prepare song, and download the instructions to check them before reviewing an inscription.

For detailed formats and constraints, see the chain device reference, historical import reference and instrument reference.
