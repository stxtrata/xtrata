No separate permission is needed for these CC0 sounds. Attribution is retained as a courtesy.

- **applause.mp3**: [Applause in a large hall or church](https://opengameart.org/content/applause-in-a-large-hall-or-church), by eXpl0it3r. First 4.5 seconds, edge fades, stereo MP3 at 96 kbit/s.
