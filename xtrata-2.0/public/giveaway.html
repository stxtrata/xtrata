    What is Xtrata Music?

    Xtrata Music is a radio where the music belongs to the people who made and hold it. Listen for 30 seconds and the song pays its holder a small amount, straight away. Like a song and that is recorded too. Albums are sent as links, so a gift can be as simple as a text message.

    For the crypto-curious: what is actually happening
    • The songs are inscribed on Xtrata, on the Stacks network, and paid plays are small STX transfers to the song’s holder.
    • Your listening wallet is a real Stacks wallet created from a passkey (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello). The passkey never leaves your device, and there is no seed phrase.
    • Claiming a link sends a small starter balance to a brand-new wallet. Each album goes into its own numbered wallet under your passkey.
    • Already on Stacks? You can connect Xverse or Leather on the radio and listen with your own wallet.
