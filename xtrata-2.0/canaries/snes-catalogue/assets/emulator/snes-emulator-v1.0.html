Display

Output is always 4:3. Hi-res (512-wide) and interlaced modes are rendered at full resolution.

Keyboard (player 1)

Click a key, then press the new key. P pause, M mute, hold Tab to fast-forward.

Gamepads

No gamepad detected. Press a button on a connected pad to wake it up. Pad 1 is added to the keyboard; pad 2 drives player 2.
