Support as you listen

Each eligible start pays 0.00005 STX to the current song holder, plus the network fee. Xtrata takes no platform fee.

Payment details

Fees never increase automatically. Payments may wait for confirmation; music continues free while they do. Those free starts are never charged later.

How your local wallet works

The app stores a signing key on this computer, outside your browser. You don’t need an external wallet popup for each song. Losing these local files can lose the balance; software with access to them can spend it. We recommend keeping no more than 1 STX here.

Keep this app running and your computer awake. Closing or reloading the page switches support off. Free listening remains available. A paid start records the start, not a full listen.

Take your funds back

Request a return whenever you like. The app must be running and pending payments must resolve first. Nothing is sent until you review and approve it.

Enter the address you want to receive the funds. We never assume the deposit sender is yours. Default fee: 300 microSTX (0.0003 STX); it is not increased automatically.

Return excess leaves exactly 1 STX after the fee. Return all sends the remaining balance minus the fee, including the listening reserve.
